## Confirm what is arranged
Keep the appointment details, the scope discussed and the provider contact available. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed appointment.

## Arrange access
Nominate someone who can provide authorised access and explain site requirements. Discuss any interruption to normal activities before the visit.

## Keep the outcome
Ask for the work record and any outstanding recommendations. Confirm which items require a separate quotation or follow-up visit.

If the issue needs assessment first, see [repair assessment](/services/repair-assessment/). For changes to existing equipment or property, see [planned improvements](/services/planned-improvements/).
