## Start with the outcome
Explain what you need and why. For a fault, describe what changed and when you first noticed it. A useful enquiry distinguishes what you observed from what you suspect caused it.

## Gather the details you already have
Include the site area, an access contact and equipment make or model if known. Share available records. Take photos only from a normal, safe area; do not dismantle equipment to improve a photograph.

## Ask about the next step
Confirm whether an assessment is needed, what it may cost and who will arrange access. Ask which documents or photographs would help before the visit.

For maintenance, read about [equipment maintenance](/services/equipment-maintenance/). For a current fault, see [repair assessment](/services/repair-assessment/).
