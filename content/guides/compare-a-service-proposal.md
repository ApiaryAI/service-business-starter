## Compare like with like
Check the work, materials, visits and records each proposal includes. A lower total may cover less work. Ask for unclear exclusions to be explained before you decide.

## Confirm the practical arrangements
Identify access responsibilities, scheduling constraints and how additional work will be approved. Keep the agreed proposal and any changes together.

## Check the fit for the job
Ask about relevant experience and qualifications for your actual project. Do not assume an example on a website confirms support for every equipment model or site.
